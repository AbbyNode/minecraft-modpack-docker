# Job Application Management - Complete Instructions

## Overview
This is a full-time job application program. You are required to work **at the local library** - no work from home. Minimum 40 hours per week, 7 days a week for 3-4 weeks.

## Goal
Apply to **140 jobs per week minimum** to maximize your chances of getting hired.

---

## DAY 1: Resume Update

### Tasks
1. Update your resume with latest experience, skills, and education
2. Tailor it for software development roles (primary target)
3. Create variations for:
   - Software/Database roles
   - Teaching positions
   - Hardware/IT roles
   - Retail positions
4. Submit resume to manager for review
5. Make any requested changes

**Deliverable:** Finalized resume approved by manager

---

## DAY 2 and BEYOND: Job Applications

### Daily Requirements
- **Location:** Work at the local library (required)
- **Hours:** 8 hours per day minimum
- **Application Rate:** 2-4 jobs per hour
- **Daily Goal:** 16-32 applications per day
- **Weekly Goal:** 140+ applications

### Job Application Platforms (Rotate Daily)
Use these platforms and rotate through them each day to find fresh postings:
1. **Indeed** (indeed.com)
2. **Monster** (monster.com)
3. **Magnet** (magnet.com or theMuse.com if Magnet unavailable)

**Daily Rotation Example:**
- Day 2 (Monday): Start with Indeed
- Day 3 (Tuesday): Start with Monster
- Day 4 (Wednesday): Start with Magnet
- Day 5 (Thursday): Start with Indeed again
- Continue rotating...

## Step-by-Step Daily Workflow

### Morning Setup (15 minutes)
1. Arrive at the library
2. Find a quiet workspace with good internet
3. Log into your computer
4. Open your daily checklist template
5. Open your job links template
6. Start your timer for hour tracking

### Application Process (Repeat all day)

#### STEP 1: Software Jobs (Hours 1-5)
1. Go to **[Platform of the day]** (rotate: Indeed → Monster → Magnet)
2. Search for "Software Developer" + your location
3. Apply filters:
   - Job Type: Full-time
   - Experience Level: All that match your experience
4. Open 5-10 job postings in new tabs
5. For each job:
   - Read the description quickly (2-3 minutes)
   - If it matches your skills: Apply immediately
   - If uncertain: Apply anyway (you need volume)
   - Copy job link to your JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE
   - Mark application complete in your checklist
6. Repeat with different search terms every hour
7. **Goal:** Apply to 14-16 software jobs in these 5 hours

#### STEP 2: Teaching Jobs (Hour 5-6)
1. Switch to a different platform than you used for software
2. Search for "Computer Science Instructor" or "Professor"
3. Apply filters:
   - Job Type: Full-time
   - Contract Type: Permanent (NOT temporary)
4. For each teaching job found:
   - Verify it's NOT part-time or temporary
   - Verify it's at a legitimate educational institution
   - Apply if it meets requirements
   - Copy job link to your JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE
5. **Goal:** Apply to 2 teaching jobs in this hour

#### STEP 3: Hardware/IT Jobs (Hour 6-7)
1. Switch platforms again
2. Search for "IT Support" or "Hardware Technician"
3. Apply filters:
   - Job Type: Full-time
4. For each hardware job:
   - Quick skim of requirements
   - Apply if it's full-time
   - Copy job link to your JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE
5. **Goal:** Apply to 3 hardware jobs in this hour

#### STEP 4: More Software Jobs (Hour 7-8)
1. Return to software job searches
2. Use different search terms than morning
3. Try second or third platform
4. **Goal:** Apply to 4-5 more software jobs

#### STEP 5: Retail Jobs (Last 30 min)
1. Search for "Retail Sales" or "Store Manager"
2. Apply filters:
   - Job Type: Full-time
3. **Goal:** Apply to 2 retail jobs

### End of Day (15 minutes)
1. Update your DAILY_CHECKLIST with final counts
2. Update your WEEKLY_CHECKLIST with progress
3. Save all job links in JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE
4. Fill out your WEEKLY_HOURS_TEMPLATE with today's hours
5. Review tomorrow's platform rotation

### End of Each Week
1. Complete your WEEKLY_HOURS_TEMPLATE

2. Complete your WEEKLY_CHECKLIST
   - Verify you hit 140+ applications
   - Verify job type quotas:
     - ✓ 100+ Software jobs
     - ✓ 10+ Teaching jobs
     - ✓ 20+ Hardware jobs
     - ✓ 10+ Retail jobs
3. Submit to manager:
   - Weekly hours report
   - Weekly checklist
   - Job links document
4. Meet with manager for review

### If You're Behind on Quota
- Work overtime to catch up
- Focus on the job categories you're behind on
- Increase application rate to 4+ per hour
- Extend daily hours beyond 8 hours

## Time Management Tips

1. **Set hourly alarms** - Check if you're on pace (2-4 apps/hour)
2. **Use templates** - Have cover letter templates ready
3. **Don't overthink** - Apply quickly, perfect is the enemy of good
4. **Take short breaks** - 5 minutes every hour
5. **Stay focused** - No social media during work hours
6. **Ask for help** - Contact manager if you're stuck
