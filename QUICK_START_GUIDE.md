# Job Application Program - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome!

This is your complete job application management system. You have 3-4 weeks to maximize your job applications and land a position. This guide will help you get started quickly.

---

You've been provided with 5 documents:

1. **JOB_APPLICATION_INSTRUCTIONS.md** - Complete instructions for the entire program
2. **DAILY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md** - Use this every single day
3. **WEEKLY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md** - Track your weekly progress

4. **Create your resume(s)**:

## 📅 Day 2+: Application Days

### Every Morning Routine

1. **Go to the library** (NO working from home!)
2. **Bring with you:**
   - Laptop/device
   - All template documents (printed or digital)
   - Your approved resume(s)
   - Phone charger
   - Water/snacks
3. **Set up your workspace** at the library
4. **Open these documents:**
   - DAILY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md (fill out today's date)
   - JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE.md (to log applications)
   - WEEKLY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md (to update progress)
5. **Start your timer** to track hours

### Daily Work Schedule (8 hours)

**Follow DAILY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md exactly**

| Time | Activity | Goal |
|------|----------|------|
| Hour 1-5 | Apply to Software Jobs | 14-16 apps |
| Hour 6 | Apply to Teaching Jobs | 1-2 apps |
| Hour 7 | Apply to Hardware Jobs | 2-3 apps |
| Hour 8 | Software + Retail Jobs | 3-4 apps |

**Daily Total:** 20-25 applications

### Platform Rotation

**Rotate which platform you start with each day:**
- Day 2 (Mon): Indeed
- Day 3 (Tue): Monster  
- Day 4 (Wed): Magnet
- Day 5 (Thu): Indeed (back to start)
- Continue rotating...

### End of Day Routine

1. **Update DAILY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md** with final counts
2. **Update WEEKLY_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md** with today's progress
3. **Save all job links** in JOB_LINKS_TEMPLATE.md
4. **Log hours** in WEEKLY_HOURS_TEMPLATE.md
5. **Pack up and leave** the library

## 🎯 Application Strategy

### How to Apply Fast (2-4 jobs per hour)

1. **Search** with the right terms (see instructions)
2. **Filter** for full-time only
3. **Open 5-10 jobs** in new tabs
4. **Quick scan** the description (2-3 minutes)
5. **Apply immediately** - don't overthink!
6. **Copy the job link** to your tracking template
7. **Move on** to the next job

### Don't Waste Time On:
- ❌ Perfecting your cover letter for each job
- ❌ Reading every detail of the job description
- ❌ Worrying if you meet 100% of requirements
- ❌ Social media or distractions

### Do Focus On:
- ✅ Volume - apply to as many as possible
- ✅ Full-time permanent positions only
- ✅ Tracking every application
- ✅ Meeting your hourly quota (2-4 apps/hour)
